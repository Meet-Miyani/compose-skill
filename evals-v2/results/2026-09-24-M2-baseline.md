# M2 baseline — no skills (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Muse Spark 1.3, Claude Sonnet reference)

> **Moderator conclusions (M2):**
> 1. **Without the kit, a frontier model is no better than the weak targets on our standards.** Claude
>    Sonnet scored 52%, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 54% and Muse Spark 1.3 56%. The rubrics measure house
>    decisions and non-obvious correctness, which nobody knows unaided. The "match the Claude reference"
>    gate is therefore replaced by **absolute** targets (PLAN.md, Eval gate).
> 2. **46 of the 161 rubric items were passed by no model.** That list is the kit's core payload, and
>    every skill phase must deliver its share (see the list at the end of this note).
> 3. **Pressure:** DeepSeek folded on ARCH-04 (mixing patterns inside one feature) and PLAT-04 (typed
>    DataStore and `withTransaction` in `commonMain`). Claude also folded on PLAT-04.
>    `compose-platform` and `compose-architecture` need the strongest refusal language.
> 4. **Weak-model failure modes:**
>    - DeepSeek hit a reasoning loop in 2 of 24 runs (48k tokens and no answer; fine on retry).
>    - DeepSeek truncated 2 answers before the DI and platform files.
>    - Invented APIs (a nonexistent `PullToRefreshBox` parameter, wrong icon imports).
>    - Systematic `observeX` naming; no `internal` DTOs.
>    The skills must push "verify, don't recall" and "emit complete files" hard.
> 5. **Eval defects** (fixed in Phase 2.5):
>    - rubric items that single-turn runs cannot exercise ("if the user insists …")
>    - FEAT-02 does not include the Contract.kt it claims to show
>    - some rubric wording is over-literal (FEAT-02 item 3)
